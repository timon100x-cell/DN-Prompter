# King of Nothing: the king in his ruin (no crown)

Same setting: break of dawn, smouldering ruin, small fires, the whole sky thick black smoke.
Each shot is two steps: first the empty plate, then add the king. New Grok chat for every step. 16:9.
King sheet = the skeleton king's character sheet (blue robe). He never wears the crown in these shots.
Check every frame: smooth skull, no cracks or marks.

## K1. On his knees

Step 1, plate. Attach: picture A (throne hall ruin).
```
Move the camera into the middle of the ruin, at eye level, looking across an open stretch of grey ash surrounded by fallen black beams and charred wall stumps, the burned throne small and blurred in the background. Leave a clear open space of ash in the center of the frame. A few small orange fires flicker in the rubble, thin smoke drifts. The whole sky is filled with thick black smoke, a thin band of pale dawn light at the horizon. Keep the ruin, the throne and the sky of Image 1. Nobody in the scene.
```

Step 2, add the king. Attach: 1 your plate, 2 king sheet.
```
Add the skeleton king from Image 2 to Image 1, kneeling alone in the open ash in the center, both knees in the ash, his head bowed low, his arms hanging limp at his sides. He wears no crown: his bare skull is smooth, intact and clean with no marks. His faded blue brocade robe spreads around him in the ash, its hem blackened, ash on his fur collar. Kneeling, his head is at about the height of the charred wall stumps around him. Use Image 2 only for his skull, robe and fur collar, not its light or pose.
```
