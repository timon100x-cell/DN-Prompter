# King of Nothing: lyrics (Dark Nord)

Verse 1: Have you heard about the castle? / Standing in the meadow? / A king upon his throne / Who guards a wealth unknown / They say he hides a fortune / A king's worth of gold / But no one gets inside / No matter what they're told

Pre-chorus: (SOME!) came bearing silver / (SOME!) came bearing swords / (SOME!) came asking nicely / (SOME!) came demanding more / But he never gave an answer / Never opened the door / And every time they left him / The rumors grew more

Chorus: He's the lord of the meadow / The master of the keep / A fortune lies beneath him / A secret buried deep / They can beg and they can plead / They can offer any price / But the king will guard his treasure / And he won't think twice!

Bridge: They searched beneath the stone / They tore the ruins wide / No jewels were found below / No riches left inside / No silver in the ashes / No gold beneath the ground / Just broken beams and rubble... / (whispered) And silence all around / NOTHING!

Final chorus: He's the KING OF NOTHING! / No gold was buried there / He built his mighty legend / With whispers in the air / They burned the walls for treasure / But found no royal prize / His only wealth was watching / The wonder in their eyes!

Outro: The king of nothing / No riches inside the walls / Just one old wooden throne / And stories widely spread / He ruled the minds of others / With nothing in his head
