# Video bible: King of Nothing

Started: 13 November 2026. Current stage: 1 World.

## World

- **Premise:** A lone king guards a castle on the prairie, rumored to hide a fortune. Crowds come with silver, swords and finally torches; they burn it and find nothing but one old wooden throne.
- **Feeling:** sly, grim triumph. He won by letting them believe.
- **Cast:** the skeleton cowboy as the king. The treasure seekers are an unnamed crowd (no sheets).
- **Scope:** not decided yet
- **Place and time:** a western frontier world. A weathered castle built entirely of grey timber (logs and planks, with patches of cracked whitewash), faded crimson wooden-shingle conical tower roofs, a small moat and a wooden plank bridge, alone on flat open prairie. Timber, not stone. Late autumn, pale dry brown grass, overcast pale sky, low mystic fog hovering just above the ground. Inspiration image: `refs/castle-inspiration.jpg` (Tim's own Grok image; its layout and mood, but the walls become timber).
- **Light rules:** Day scenes: soft even overcast light from a flat pale sky, fog glowing white, subjects clearly readable. Fire and ruin scenes: firelight is the only warm light, everything else stays cold grey.
- **Palette:** faded crimson (the king's coat and the castle roofs only) against pale grey sky, bone white, grey timber and dry brown grass. Red is reserved for the king and his castle. Fire orange only in the burning scenes.
- **Camera rules:** film feel changes per shot (Tim's choice); see the look line section.
- **Motifs:** the crown, the one old wooden throne, the closed gate, torches, the low fog.
- **Never appears:** real treasure: no real gold, silver, jewels or valuables. Gold paint (for example on the crown or throne) is fine.
