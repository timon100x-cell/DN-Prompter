# Hide: mood and color test (27 Nov 2026)

The same ruined casino hall five times. Only the light, the colors and the film feel change. New chat for each. 16:9, 2K. No attachments.

## 1. Gaslight gold

```
The ruined casino hall deep inside an old, rotting riverboat at night, long abandoned, seen from the entrance, an enclosed room with no windows. Three round card tables with torn, faded felt stand across the room with scattered playing cards and poker chips, one wooden chair lies broken on its side on the warped, rotting floorboards, and a roulette table stands further back. The long carved bar on the right sags and splits under a few dusty bottles, the wall panels are peeling and water-stained, the curtains hang in shredded, moth-eaten strips, and cobwebs hang from a crooked chandelier. Warm, smoky lamplight from a few brass oil lamps on the bar and tables throws amber pools of light and long shadows, every surface still readable. Amber gold and dark blood-red against deep brown-black wood. Wide shot at eye level from the entrance, the nearest card table sharp and the back of the room softly out of focus. Photoreal film still with natural surface texture, everything in the focal plane sharp. Shot on 35mm film with fine grain.
```
