# The skeleton cowboy

Saved character for Dark Nord. He is always a skeleton. His outfit may change from video to video.

## Identity block (never changes)

`A tall skeleton cowboy with a weathered bone-white skull, a cracked forehead with thin glowing red fissures, and empty dark eye sockets.`

Notes:

- He has no skin, lips or eyes, so he never needs an expression sheet. Emotion comes from pose, head tilt and hands.
- The red fissures are thin and dim. Write them once per prompt. Never write "glowing" for anything else in the same prompt, or the glow spreads.
- Pose energy: powerful, defiant, in command of the scene. Never passive or slumped unless Tim asks.

## Outfits

One sheet per outfit. Add a new entry here whenever Tim approves a new outfit.

### Outfit 1: classic duster

- **Outfit block:** `He wears a broken-brimmed dark cowboy hat with a few long strands of grey hair beneath it, a tattered brown leather duster, a dark bandana around his neck, black leather gloves, and one silver sheriff star on the left side of his chest.`
- **Short anchor:** `the skeleton cowboy in his tattered brown leather duster and broken-brimmed hat`
- **Sheet:** not made yet. Make it with the two steps in the skill's `characters.md` the first time he is used, then save it here as `classic-duster-sheet` and note the date.
